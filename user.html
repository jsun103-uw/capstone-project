The User

As a player in the foxhole and io game niche, you can experience the group-combat of foxhole with lower stakes and in an arcade style. Foxhole has a massive persistent world and economy where every bullet, weapon, and vehicle you use was created and delivered by someone to the front. This can feel daunting, as if you want to pilot a tank, you often have to spend hours or a day yourself creating one. This is part of the appeal to foxhole, but if you ever wanted to focus on combat without the stakes of losing hours of your own work or the work of someone else, Open Trenches may be the game for you.

The Product

Open Trenches is a casual io game designed with the group-battle dynamics of games such as foxhole. The systems are much more straightfoward and the stakes are much lower. You can entrench positions to defend against enemy attacks and assault entrenched positions or maneuver around them in cooperation with dozens of players and bots. Become a cog in a machine, and play as an ordinary soldier where outcomes are decided by many and not by one.

The Features

  • Fight with trench warfare; entrench and defend positions and assault entrenched positions.
  • Cooperate with other players and bots in large scale coordinated efforts.
  • Purchase new weapons and activated abilities using per-game currency.
