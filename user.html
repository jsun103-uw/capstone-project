The User

As a player in the foxhole or io game niche, you can experience the trench warfare combat of Foxhole in the hypercasual arcade style of io games. You can entrench positions to defend against enemy attacks and assault entrenched positions or maneuver around them in cooperation with dozens of players and bots.

Features

  • Fight with trench warfare; entrench and defend positions and assault entrenched positions.
  • Cooperate with other players and bots in large scale coordinated efforts.
  • Purchase new weapons and activated abilities using in-game currency.
